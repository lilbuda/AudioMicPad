# Changelog

Installers and full release notes are available on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/lilbuda/AudioMicPad/releases).

## [1.2.0](https://github.com/lilbuda/AudioMicPad/releases/tag/v1.2.0) — 2026-11-26

- Added live microphone pitch control from −12 to +12 semitones. Music and sound effects keep their original pitch.
- Added elapsed time, total duration, and a seekable timeline. Seeking works during playback and while paused.

## [1.1.3](https://github.com/lilbuda/AudioMicPad/releases/tag/v1.1.3) — 2026-08-29

- Double-clicking a song switches tracks, and the transport controls work for songs started from the list.
- Play resumes a paused track from its saved position.
- Updated the app icon and added links to the website and GitHub Sponsors.

## [1.1.1](https://github.com/lilbuda/AudioMicPad/releases/tag/v1.1.1) — 2026-08-24

- Raised the microphone gain limit from 150% to 500% and explained the risk of noise and distortion at high gain.
- Included the in-app help guide, themes, custom global shortcuts, and separate music volumes for the virtual microphone and headset.
