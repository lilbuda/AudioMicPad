# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Keep disagreements focused on the work and offer constructive feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted insults, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project discussions.

Use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report abusive content or behavior. Keep personal details out of public threads. The maintainer may remove abusive content, lock discussions, or restrict participation.
