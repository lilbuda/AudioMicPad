AudioMicPad third-party notices

VB-CABLE
--------

AudioMicPad is designed to work with the standard VB-CABLE virtual audio driver.
VB-CABLE is not included in AudioMicPad or its installer. Download it from its
official publisher, VB-Audio Software, at:

https://vb-audio.com/Cable/

VB-CABLE is donationware. If you find it useful, please consider supporting
VB-Audio. Installation requires administrator rights and Windows should be
restarted afterward. Licensing information is available at:

https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm
